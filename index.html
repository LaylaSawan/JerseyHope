Our Story

Sport has the power to unite the world.

My name is Layla. I'm a Grade 10 student and a referee with the Oakridge Optimist Soccer Club in London, Ontario. Every season, I watch kids finish their last game, fold up their jerseys, and never think about them again.

Meanwhile, on the other side of the world in villages in the West Bank, children have the same passion for the sport, but they don't have the gear.

So I decided to do something about it. Jersey Hope started with a simple idea: what if the jersey you no longer need could give another child the joy of belonging to a team?

This initiative is run entirely by me, from London, Ontario with the help of our community and the generosity of families like yours!

"Sport doesn't care where you're from. A ball, a field, and a jersey, that's all a child needs to feel like they belong."
Layla Founder, Jersey Hope · Referee, Oakridge Optimist Soccer Club
How It Works

Simple. Transparent. Impactful.

01

Season Ends

At the close of the sports season, I will reach out to house-league team coaches and parents, starting with U6, U8, and U10 in the Oakridge Optimist Soccer Club to collect gently used jerseys and shorts.

02

We Collect & Sort

Donated gear is collected and packed for shipment. Every item is checked to ensure it's truly usable and meaningful to receive.

03

Delivered With Care

Gear is shipped to underserved schools and communities, beginning in villages in the West Bank, through trusted local connections. Children receive the gear directly.
